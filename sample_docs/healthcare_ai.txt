Artificial Intelligence in Modern Healthcare
Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning have transformed modern healthcare systems worldwide.
In diagnostic imaging, deep learning models can detect early signs of diabetic retinopathy, pneumonia, and oncology markers from MRI and CT scans with accuracy matching or exceeding human experts.
Beyond imaging, AI accelerates drug discovery by simulating molecular interactions in days rather than years.
Furthermore, natural language processing assists clinical documentation, reducing physician burnout and enabling healthcare practitioners to focus on patient-centered care.
However, ethical challenges such as algorithmic bias, data privacy, and explainability must be addressed to ensure safe, equitable, and responsible clinical deployment.
